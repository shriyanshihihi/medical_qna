SYNTHETIC DEMO TEXT (written for this project, not from a real textbook).

Asthma. Asthma is a chronic disease of the airways in which they become inflamed and narrow, causing wheeze, cough, chest tightness and breathlessness, often worse at night or with exercise. A short-acting beta-2 agonist inhaler such as salbutamol relieves attacks quickly. Inhaled corticosteroids are the main preventer medicine because they reduce airway inflammation. Peak flow measurement and spirometry help in diagnosis and monitoring.

Pneumonia. Pneumonia is an infection of the lung tissue. Patients have fever, cough with sputum, chest pain on breathing and shortness of breath. A chest X-ray usually shows a patch of consolidation. Bacterial pneumonia is treated with antibiotics such as amoxicillin, and severe cases need hospital care and oxygen.

Tuberculosis. Tuberculosis is caused by Mycobacterium tuberculosis and mainly affects the lungs. Typical symptoms are a cough lasting more than three weeks, night sweats, fever and weight loss. Standard treatment combines four drugs, isoniazid, rifampicin, pyrazinamide and ethambutol, for the first two months, followed by a longer continuation phase.
